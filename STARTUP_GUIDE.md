## 1. Legal Entity Setup

### Which Structure? Private Limited vs LLP

| Feature | LLP | Private Limited (Pvt Ltd) |
|---|---|---|
| **Best For** | Bootstrapped, no investor plans | Scalable, investor-ready |
| **Registration Cost** | ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 | ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 |
| **Annual Compliance** | ₹3k – ₹15k/year | ₹25k – ₹75k/year |
| **Fundraising** | ❌ Difficult (no equity shares) | ✅ Excellent (equity, ESOPs) |
| **DPIIT Eligible** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Tax Holiday (80-IAC)** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |

> **💡 RECOMMENDATION:** Start as **Private Limited Company** if you plan to raise funds within 12–24 months. Converting LLP → Pvt Ltd later costs ₹10,000+ and may lose benefits.

### How to Register a Private Limited Company

**Portal:** [MCA V3 Portal](https://www.mca.gov.in)

**Documents Required:**
- Aadhaar + PAN of all directors (min 2)
- Passport-size photos of directors
- Address proof of registered office (rent agreement + NOC + utility bill)
- Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for all directors
- Director Identification Number (DIN) — applied during SPICe+ form

**Steps:**
1. Obtain DSC for all directors (~₹1,500 each, 2 days)
2. File **SPICe+ Form** (Single Point of Contact) on MCA — includes Name Reservation + Incorporation + DIN + PAN + TAN + GSTIN + EPFO + ESIC
3. Draft MOA (Memorandum) and AOA (Articles) — use standard templates
4. Pay government fees (~₹2,000–₹5,000 based on authorized capital)
5. Receive Certificate of Incorporation (3–7 working days)
6. Open a current bank account with CoI + PAN + AOA/MOA

**Cost:** ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 (including professional fees)

## 2. Government Registrations

### 2.1 DPIIT Startup Recognition (FREE — Most Important)

This is the **gateway** to all government benefits: tax holidays, seed funding, IP rebates, and cloud credits.

**Eligibility:**
- Private Limited / LLP / Registered Partnership
- Less than 10 years old
- Annual turnover < ₹100 crore
- Working on innovation/technology-driven product
- Not formed by splitting an existing business

**Documents Required:**
- Certificate of Incorporation
- PAN Card of the entity
- Pitch deck / business plan describing innovation
- Directors' details (mobile, email, ID proof)
- Authorization letter (if applicable)

**Steps:**
1. Go to [startupindia.gov.in](https://www.startupindia.gov.in) → Create account
2. Complete startup profile
3. Click "Get Recognised" → Redirects to [NSWS Portal](https://www.nsws.gov.in)
4. Fill form + upload documents
5. Receive DPIIT Recognition Certificate (2–7 days)

**Cost:** ₹0 (completely free)

### 2.2 MSME Udyam Registration (FREE)

**Portal:** [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)

**Benefits:** Collateral-free loans, delayed payment protection, government tender preference, subsidy on patents/trademarks.

**Steps:**
1. Visit portal → Enter Aadhaar of founder
2. Enter PAN → Portal auto-fetches GST/IT data
3. Fill enterprise details + NIC code (select IT/Software services)
4. Submit → Receive Udyam Registration Number instantly

**Cost:** ₹0 | **Time:** 15 minutes

### 2.3 GST Registration

**When Required:**
- Turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states)
- OR you provide inter-state services (mandatory regardless of turnover)
- OR you want to claim Input Tax Credit from B2B clients

**SaaS GST Rate:** 18%

**Recommendation:** Register voluntarily even below threshold — B2B clients prefer GST-registered vendors and you can claim ITC on hosting, software, and equipment expenses.

**Portal:** [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in) — apply via Form REG-01

## 6. Tax Benefits

### 6.1 Section 80-IAC Tax Holiday

- **Benefit:** 100% tax deduction on profits for 3 consecutive years (chosen from first 10 years)
- **Eligibility:** DPIIT-recognized Pvt Ltd / LLP, turnover < ₹100 crore
- **How to Apply:** After DPIIT recognition → apply to Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) via Startup India portal with financial documents

### 6.2 Angel Tax — ABOLISHED ✅

As of April 2025, Section 56(2)(viib) "Angel Tax" has been **completely abolished** for all companies. No more tax on share premiums from investors.

### 6.3 Other Benefits for DPIIT Startups

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| **IP Fee Rebate** | 80% rebate on patent filing, 50% on trademark filing |
| **Self-Certification** | Under 6 labour laws and 3 environmental laws |
| **Easy Winding Up** | Fast-track 90-day closure process |
| **Public Procurement** | Relaxed norms for government tenders |
| **Carry Forward Losses** | Section 79 relaxation on change in shareholding |

## Appendix C: Financial Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue — predictable monthly income |
| **Churn** | % of customers who cancel per month (target < 5%) |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost — what you spend to get one customer |
| **LTV** | Lifetime Value — total revenue from one customer over their lifetime |
| **ARR** | Annual Recurring Revenue = MRR × 12 |
| **Non-Dilutive** | Funding that doesn't require giving up equity (grants, debt) |
| **Convertible Note** | Debt that converts to equity during next funding round |
